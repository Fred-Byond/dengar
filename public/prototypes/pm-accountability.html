Verification & the citizen loop

✅ Verification queue

ministry says done · PMO must verify with evidence before it closes
A ministry marking its own work complete is a claim, not an outcome. Nothing reaches “delivered” — and no citizen is notified — until the PMO Delivery Unit verifies the evidence.

🔎 Citizen follow-ups

by session reference · counter, WhatsApp and portal

Follow-up volume by concern

Follow-up volume is itself a signal. A concern citizens keep chasing is a concern they do not believe has been addressed — regardless of what the delivery status says.

⚖️ Disputed & reassigned

ministry contests ownership or feasibility
A tracking system that cannot record disagreement forces ministries to go silent instead. Dispute is a legitimate state with a resolution path, not a failure.

🔒 Protected disclosure referrals

acknowledgement tracking only · never assigned to a ministry
Why these are tracked differently. Protected disclosures are referred to the competent authority through the statutory channel, never routed to a ministry and never assigned to a named officer in this system. What is tracked is only whether the receiving authority has acknowledged the referral — so the PMO can confirm the channel is working without ever seeing what was disclosed, who disclosed it, or who was named.
