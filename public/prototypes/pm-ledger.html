🗣️ What this cohort actually asked for

request-actionability signals, ranked
This is the bridge from thousands of sessions to a single directive. A cohort is not a report — select it, read what it asked for, and issue one directive against the whole cohort so every session in it inherits the outcome.
Learning loop

🧪 Calibration queue

human labelling improves the rubric, not the model
What “learning” means here. Human labels on this queue are compared against the scorer to measure agreement and refine the CVIF rubric, the Manglish glossary and the confidence thresholds. Citizen content is never used to train or fine-tune a model — not ours, not a subprocessor's. That is a contractual commitment, so the learning loop is deliberately built to improve the rubric rather than the weights.

📚 Research corpus

consent-gated · de-identified
Only sessions where the citizen opted in at booking enter the corpus, and they enter de-identified. A citizen can withdraw consent at any time using their reference number, which removes the session from the corpus without affecting the aggregate trends already published.
