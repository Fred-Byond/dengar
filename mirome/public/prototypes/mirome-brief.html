Meridian Group — what your people are telling you

Logistics & customer operations · 312 employees · 7 departments · Kuala Lumpur, Penang, Johor

Assessed82 of 96 invited (85.4%)
MethodConfidential digital-human interview + 10-item pulse + 2 scenarios
Routed to a reviewer2 sessions
Read timeAbout ten minutes

What the data says

derived from 82 assessments, not written by hand

Ability to absorb change is this organisation's strength at 75.3; ability to deliver is the constraint at 54, with 72.3% of responses not positive and collaboration the weakest link inside it.

On constructive conflict, people who manage people rate this organisation 18.8 points higher than the people who report to them. That distance is itself the finding: it is the measure of how much of what is happening below is reaching the people deciding above.

Start inside 30 days

Leaders answer the three hardest questions from this survey, in public

Take the three lowest-rated statements and have the executive committee respond to each in an all-hands — what they accept, what they dispute, and what they will change. No anonymous Q&A, no written memo.

Because: People can explain their ideas, but junior staff hold back from disagreeing with senior colleagues. The issue is upward communication and safety, not speaking ability. (Psychological safety · 68% of participants affected · high confidence)

Expected move+3 to +6 index points by day 30
Measured by: Psychological safety index at day 30, plus whether staff can name one thing leadership changed
Not: Running a values workshop. People do not doubt the values; they doubt what happens when they speak.

What this cannot tell you

stated, not buried

Every limit below is enforced in the pipeline, not promised in a policy document. Participants are shown the same list before they consent — which is a large part of why the answers are worth reading.

  • This measures how the organisation is experienced, not how any individual performs. No score here belongs to a named employee.
  • It cannot tell you who said what. Groups below five people are never displayed, under any filter.
  • It is a diagnosis, not a prediction. It does not forecast attrition, revenue or performance, and should not be presented as if it does.
  • Movement under 2.5 index points is inside the noise for a sample this size and is reported as steady.
  • Accent, grammar, fluency, appearance and connection quality are excluded from every score. A participant is never marked down for how they speak.
